## Overall read

This is a single-family home (roughly **1,670 sq ft on the main floor** per A2's callout) with what looks like a full second storey or loft over at least part of the footprint (per the two-story cut in A5's Section 1), an attached garage (per the foundation plan's double-hatched slab area and the fire-rated wall type on A3), brick veneer on at least the front and both side/corner elevations with a stone or masonry wainscot accent at the front entry, and a mono-pitch/shed roof condition at the back (per A5 Section 2's roofline). The set is internally consistent — same address, same lot/block, same title block style — and together with the foundation plan's engineer's stamp, this reads as a complete, permit-set bundle rather than a partial or draft set.

## Quality note

These captures came from zooming into a screenshot of the Notes app rather than exporting the original scan file, because the native macOS save-sheet for exporting the note as a PDF got stuck in a verification loop I couldn't get past safely. That's good enough to document structure, room layout, and general assembly — which is what's above — but the small printed callouts (exact stud spacing, insulation R-values, footing dimensions, individual room dimension strings) are at or below the resolution where I can read them reliably by eye. When we run these through BuilderAI's actual Claude vision call, it may do meaningfully better on that fine print since it'll be working from the same pixels at full resolution rather than my downsampled zoom — so that run itself will also tell us whether these capture files are good enough for the app's real use case, or whether we need a proper high-DPI scan/photo from you instead.
